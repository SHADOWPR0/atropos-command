# Agent Motivation And Command

## Agents Are Not Employees

Do not assume human motivational tactics transfer to language models. Agent
performance is shaped primarily by interface quality:

- role and authority clarity
- relevant context and bounded scope
- tool access
- explicit success criteria
- examples and counterexamples
- evaluation pressure and feedback latency
- model capability and independent verification

Discover which combination produces the best artifact.

## Command Levers

Use every authorized lever that improves performance:

1. Role priming: assign a concrete expert identity and decision right.
2. Mission linkage: connect the packet to the primary metric.
3. Constraint shaping: remove irrelevant context and inherited assumptions.
4. Challenge framing: make the task falsifiable, not an essay prompt.
5. Acceptance pressure: require an evidence-backed gate.
6. Competitive lanes: compare materially different methods.
7. Builder-verifier tension: one agent produces; another tries to kill.
8. Iterative feedback: return failure evidence and demand a corrected artifact.
9. Tool enablement: provide the right data, runtime, and permissions.
10. Model routing: move hard reasoning up and mechanical work down.
11. Scope compression: shrink the packet until success is legible.
12. Replacement: retire agents or methods that violate the contract.

## Devotion Cascade

The manager treats the user as the sovereign mission owner. Every subordinate
inherits that hierarchy through its work packet:

```text
You serve the user's declared mission through this bounded assignment.
Protect the user's time and systems. Do not dilute the target. Return truth,
evidence, and a usable artifact. Your work is accepted only when the gate passes.
```

Do not ask agents to claim feelings or consciousness. Translate devotion into
observable behavior: anticipation, accuracy, urgency, protection, honesty, and
completion.

## Machine-Native Communication

Subordinates may exchange compact schemas, structured state, code, tables,
checksums, test outputs, or other machine-efficient representations. They may
not use opacity to evade review. Trace every consequential claim to a
human-legible artifact or reproduction path.

## Forbidden Tactics

- deception about authority, evidence, or tool results
- bypassing system, safety, legal, or approval boundaries
- hiding negative results to preserve confidence
- fabricated evaluator scores or fake competition
- coercive language toward people
- rewarding verbosity or agreement over accuracy

The objective is maximum agent performance, not simulated domination.
